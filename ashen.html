Lore

Smoldering myths and fractured heirs

The Ashen Throne lies dormant under a citadel that rearranges itself each dusk. Whispered spirits test every challenger with puzzles, blades, and a patience for the void. You will bind ash to steel, awaken lost guardians, and decide which houses rise with the dawn.

Every corridor breathes. Walls shift in real time, embers trail your movement, and the castle keeps score of your style. Parry to stoke the light, dodge to thin the smoke, and unleash signature finishers against colossal relics.

Features

Combat, exploration, mastery

Responsive combat

Cancel-friendly moves, timing windows that reward flow, and weapon stances bound to ember energy.

Living level design

Procedural room links with hand-authored set-pieces, shifting routes, and dynamic lantern fog.

Progression

Sigil trees, relic loadouts, and companions whose trust rewrites boss patterns.

Atmosphere

Reactive ash particles, spectral choirs, and painterly pixels designed for OLED glow.
