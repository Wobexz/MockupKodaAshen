Story

When the crown dims, the ash remembers

Traverse an empire turned to charcoal. Embers whisper ancient oaths as you unearth memories of the lost sovereigns, crafting runic blades and making alliances with wandering spirits.

Features

Exploration

Progression

Forge sigils that mutate abilities, bind spirit companions, and decide which relics to awaken or let slumber.

Atmosphere

Dynamic ash storms, bioluminescent flora, and a score that bends with your heartbeat keep the world alive.
